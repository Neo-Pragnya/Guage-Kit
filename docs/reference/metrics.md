# Metric catalogue

> [!NOTE]
> Generated by `scripts/gen_metric_docs.py` from the metric registry. `guage metrics list` and
> `guage metrics show <name>` print the same information for the installed version.

Guage Kit has **90 native metrics** and exposes **77 Ananke Plexus evaluators** as
metrics, for a total of 167. ↑ means higher is better, ↓ lower is better. *Sample* metrics score
each sample and report the mean with a bootstrap interval; *corpus* metrics are computed over the whole set
(their interval comes from resampling the samples).

## Native metrics

### Text (reference-based)

| metric | level | needs | parameters | description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bleu` ↑ | corpus | `output`, `expected` | `max_order=4` | Corpus BLEU (0–1): n-gram precisions pooled over all samples, with the brevity penalty. *Ref: Papineni et al. (2002); sacreBLEU.* |
| `chrf` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | `char_order=6`, `word_order=0`, `beta=2.0` | Character n-gram F-score against the best reference (0–1). ``word_order=2`` gives chrF++. *Ref: Popović (2015, 2017); sacreBLEU chrF.* |
| `chrf_pp` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | `char_order=6`, `beta=2.0`, `word_order=2` | chrF++: character 6-grams plus word bigrams. *Ref: Popović (2015, 2017); sacreBLEU chrF.* |
| `contains` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | `case_sensitive=False` | 1 when any reference appears inside the output (substring match). |
| `corpus_chrf` ↑ | corpus | `output`, `expected` | `char_order=6`, `word_order=0`, `beta=2.0` | Corpus chrF: statistics pooled over all samples (first reference). *Ref: sacreBLEU corpus chrF.* |
| `distinct_n` ↑ | corpus | `output` | `k=2` | Distinct-n: unique n-grams ÷ all n-grams over every output (diversity; ``distinct_n@1`` for unigrams). *Ref: Li et al. (2016).* |
| `exact_match` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | `strip=True`, `case_sensitive=True` | 1 when the output equals any reference exactly (after stripping whitespace). |
| `flesch_reading_ease` ↑ | sample | `output` | – | Flesch reading ease of the output (higher is easier; 60–70 is plain English). *Ref: Flesch (1948).* |
| `jaccard` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | – | Jaccard overlap of the word sets of output and the best reference. |
| `keyword_coverage` ↑ | sample | `output` | `keywords=[]` | Share of required keywords present in the output. Keywords come from the parameter or ``metadata.keywords``. |
| `length_ratio` ↓ | sample | `output`, `expected` | – | Output words ÷ reference words. 1 is the same length. Lower-is-better only when used as a maximum. |
| `levenshtein_similarity` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | – | 1 − edit distance / length of the longer string, against the closest reference (character level). |
| `normalized_match` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | – | 1 when the normalised output (lower case, no punctuation or articles) equals a normalised reference. *Ref: SQuAD v1.1 evaluation script.* |
| `numeric_match` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | `rel_tol=1e-06`, `abs_tol=1e-09`, `last=True` | 1 when the (last) number in the output equals the expected number within tolerance (math word problems). |
| `regex_match` ↑ | sample | `output` | `pattern=''`, `full=False` | 1 when the output matches the pattern (parameter, or ``metadata.pattern``). |
| `rouge` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | `variant='rougeL'`, `measure='f'`, `tokenizer='default'` | ROUGE F-measure (or precision/recall) against the best reference. | `rouge1` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | `measure='f'`, `tokenizer='default'`, `variant='rouge1'` | ROUGE-1 F-measure (unigram overlap). *Ref: Lin (2004), ROUGE.* |
| `rouge2` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | `measure='f'`, `tokenizer='default'`, `variant='rouge2'` | ROUGE-2 F-measure (bigram overlap). *Ref: Lin (2004), ROUGE.* |
| `rougeL` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | `measure='f'`, `tokenizer='default'`, `variant='rougeL'` | ROUGE-L F-measure (longest common subsequence). *Ref: Lin (2004), ROUGE.* |
| `rougeLsum` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | `measure='f'`, `tokenizer='default'`, `variant='rougeLsum'` | ROUGE-Lsum F-measure (summary-level LCS over sentences). *Ref: Lin (2004), ROUGE.* |
| `self_bleu` ↓ | corpus | `output` | `max_order=4`, `limit=200` | Mean BLEU of each output against all the others. Lower means more diverse outputs. *Ref: Zhu et al. (2018), Texygen.* |
| `sentence_bleu` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | `max_order=4` | BLEU of one output against its references, with exponential smoothing (0–1 scale). *Ref: Papineni et al. (2002); sacreBLEU exp smoothing.* |
| `token_f1` ↑ | sample | `output`, `expected` | `normalize=True` | Bag-of-words F1 between output and the best-matching reference. *Ref: SQuAD v1.1 evaluation script.* |
